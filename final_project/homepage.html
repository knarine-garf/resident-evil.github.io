Resident Evil

Resident Evil 2 Resident Evil 4 Resident Evil 7

Overview

Resident Evil is a game series made by Capcom. The first game was released in 1996 on the playstation and was monumental in defining the survival horror genre. After the first games popularity the series was continued with the release of more games. The general story of Resident evil focuses on outbreaks caused by the production and release of Biological weapons such as viruses and parasites. The corporation behind the manufacturing of these bio-weapons is the Umbrella corporation with inhumane secret experiments created by Oswell E. Spencer. Throughout the game players must fight through zombies and tyrants to survive while also managing their resources and inventory space. The games mix horror, action, exploration, and storytelling all into their games.

Leon S. Kennedy

Leon Kennedy appears in Resident Evil 2 as a rookie cop who gets recruited by the US government after the raccoon city outbreak to become a highly skilled agent tasked with the fighting of Bio Organic Weapons and the protection of high ranking members like the president.

Claire Redfield

Claire Redfield appears in Resident Evil 2 as a college student looking for her brother Chris Redfield where she both survives the raccoon city outbreak and saves a little girl in the process. She then continues to investigate and fight against organizations like Umbrella while trying to reunite with her brother Chris.

Chris Redfield

Chris Redfield first appears in the first Resident Evil game as a member of the special police unit S.T.A.R.S where he fights to survive the first major outbreak caused by the Umbrella corporation alongside Jill. He then becomes an anti-bioterrorism agent with a focus on stopping global bio-weapon threats.

Jill Valentine

Jill Valentine first appears in the first Resident Evil game along with Chris. She begins as a skilled member of S.T.A.R.S investigating the first major outbreak. She is later heavily involved in the Raccoon City outbreak and becomes a key figure in trying to stop the spread of the virus. After getting captured and saved, she joins global anti-bioterrorism efforts alongside Chris Redfield.

Ethan Winters

Ethan Winters First appears in Resident Evil 7 as an ordinary citizen who gets pulled into bio-terror events while looking for his missing wife Mia Winters. He discovers his wife in the horrifying Baker Family Estate where fights to survive against the mutated family to survive and save his wife. After having a family with Mia he is then forced into another nightmare in Resident Evil 8 where his daughter Rose is taken to a village controlled by powerful bio-engineered monsters.
